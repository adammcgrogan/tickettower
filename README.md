# Ticket Tower

A simple, good-looking ticket bot for Discord. Members open a ticket with one
click, your team answers in a private channel or thread, and everything is
managed from a clean web dashboard.

**[Add Ticket Tower to your server](https://tickettower.net/api/invite)** · [tickettower.net](https://tickettower.net)

## Getting started

1. [Add the bot](https://tickettower.net/api/invite) to your server.
2. Log in at [tickettower.net](https://tickettower.net) with Discord and pick your server.
3. Follow the quick setup on the Home page: create a ticket type, then post your
   ticket buttons in a channel.

That's it. Members click a button, and a private ticket opens for them and your
support team.

## Features

- **Ticket buttons**: post a message with buttons or a dropdown menu, designed in
  the dashboard with a live preview, with your own button colours, labels and
  images
- **Ticket types**: each opens a private channel or a private thread, with its
  own support roles, naming, welcome message and per-member limit
- **Who can open**: require or block roles per type, a wait between a member's
  tickets, and a block list for people who abuse tickets
- **Pre-ticket questions**: ask members a few questions before their ticket opens
- **Ticket actions**: claim, close with a reason, reopen, add or remove members,
  rename, from buttons or slash commands
- **Transcripts**: every ticket is saved and viewable in a Discord-style viewer,
  and searchable by what was said
- **Feedback**: members rate their ticket 1 to 5 after it closes (per type, you
  can turn this off or reword it)
- **Auto-close**: inactive tickets get a reminder, then close on their own
- **Analytics**: ticket volume and backlog, first response and resolution
  times, busiest hours, satisfaction, close reasons, and breakdowns by type
  and staff member, compared with the previous period

## Privacy

Ticket Tower stores ticket messages so it can show transcripts. See
[tickettower.net/privacy](https://tickettower.net/privacy) for what's kept and
for how long.
